OUR STORY

"WHERE SMOOTH SPIRITS
WITH A BOLD BITE"

Not Just Smoo'V started with one complaint: sandwiches were boring and smoothies were basic. So we started stacking house-baked focaccia sky-high with real ingredients, and blending real-fruit smoothies — no added colouring, no sweeteners, just fruit blended fresh. Now we do it daily from our live kitchen at AIPL Joy Central. No shortcuts, no sad lettuce — just good stuff, layered and blended right. Consider this your way to happiness.
